## What's in this release
Every sport now has a **Top list** (modelled on the CFB "JT Top 25") and a **bracket / championship view**, under **Rankings → Rankings & Brackets** (`#/rankings/lists`, `#/rankings/list/<sport>`, `#/rankings/playoffs/<sport>`). Sports: NFL, NBA, WNBA, NCAA men's basketball, NCAA women's basketball, MLB, college baseball, college softball, college gymnastics. College Football keeps its existing JT Top 25 and Bracket pages, linked from the hub.
- **Pro leagues (NFL, NBA, WNBA, MLB):** "JT Power Rankings" computed from ESPN standings with a published formula; bracket from the real postseason format (play-in, byes, series scores, live games).
- **College sports:** the official poll is shown as the JT list until a JT-authored list is dropped in (`data/jt-lists/<sport>/latest.json`); brackets/history come from NCAA.com where ESPN has gaps.
- **Honest states:** preseason, early season, offseason, final, not-available-yet, loading, error with Try again, 25 s timeout.
- Utah, BYU and Big 12 teams are highlighted (display only). Watch links stay inside the opened game view; News stays sports-only.

## Formulas
**JT Power Rankings (NFL, NBA, WNBA, MLB)** — `server/rank-lists.js`, shown in-app under "How this list works".
```
A = (W + T/2 + k/2) / (G + k)                         record, shrunk toward .500 by k phantom games
B = clamp(0.5 + (diff/G × G/(G+k)) / (5 × scale), 0, 1)  point/run differential per game, shrunk the same way
C = (last-10 wins + 1) / (last-10 games + 2)          recent form (falls back to A where no last-10 exists, e.g. NFL)
score = 100 × (0.50·A + 0.35·B + 0.15·C)

league   k   scale (points/runs per game that maps to ±0.5)
NFL      3   7 pts      thin-sample note below 4 games per team,  season = 17
NBA      8   5 pts      thin-sample note below 20 games,          season = 82
WNBA     6   4 pts      thin-sample note below 8 games,           season = 44
MLB     15   0.7 runs   thin-sample note below 30 games,          season = 162
```
Tie-breaks: win %, then point/run differential, then name. No strength-of-schedule, injury or manual adjustments. Movement (▲/▼) compares with the previous day's saved snapshot (NFL: a snapshot at least 6 days old), so it appears from the second day onward.
**College sports** — official polls (AP / Coaches for basketball, D1Baseball Top 25 for baseball via NCAA.com, ESPN.com/USA Softball poll via NCAA.com, Road to Nationals for gymnastics). A JT-authored list dropped into `data/jt-lists/<sport>/latest.json` (`{title,date,source,ranks:[{rank,team,reason}]}`) replaces the poll and the page is labelled "JT".

## Caveats (read before approving)
- **College "JT top list" is the official poll** until a JT list is supplied — nothing is invented. Gymnastics has **no bracket** (the NCAA championship is a team-score meet); the view is the championship result, history and a "how it works" card, and says so plainly.
- Gymnastics rankings come from Road to Nationals (fan-run, 30 s crawl delay, cached 24 h; first load can show "loading"); it is the **final 2026** ranking until the 2027 season starts.
- NCAA.com data comes through a third-party proxy (`ncaa-api.henrygd.me`, override with `NCAA_API_BASE`); if it is down, college baseball/softball and tournament history degrade to a not-available state (tested by A-08).
- Pro lists are record + differential + form only. The NBA is only ~4 games in, so its list is noisy and says so. NFL movement needs a 6-day-old snapshot, so none is shown yet.
- Render's disk is ephemeral: committed snapshots under `data/lists/` seed movement after a deploy; otherwise movement restarts the next day.
- The World Series is live (Brewers lead Rays 3–0; Game 4 tonight 6 PM MT) — the bracket view is a live feed and will have changed by the time it is deployed.
- The release name "Bracket Buzzer" and the 25 s client timeout are my choices.

## Defects found during QA and what was done (honest log)

**Product defects (real bugs, all fixed before the final run):**
1. *Pro teams tagged as Big 12 / Utah.* QA case N-05 showed the Bengals (Cincinnati), Texans (Houston) and Cardinals (Arizona) carrying a "Big 12" tag because the highlighter matched on city name; the Jazz (Utah) would have been tagged as the Utes. **Fixed** in `server/highlight.js` (pro franchise ids `nfl:/nba:/wnba:/mlb:` are never tagged) with a regression assertion in `tests/ncaa.test.js`.
2. *Playoff seeders accepted teams with no seed* (`null <= 7` is true in JavaScript), which could put an unseeded team into the NFL/MLB field. Found by `tests/postseason.test.js`. **Fixed** (`r.seed >= 1 && r.seed <= N`).
3. *Gymnastics history rows showed "over -- · --"* for the cancelled 2020 championship. **Fixed** in `server/feeds.js` (blank runner-up/score are dropped).
4. *UI polish found while reviewing screenshots:* a duplicate record on computed list rows, a misleading "First snapshot saved today" note, and the active sport chip being cut off in the picker. **Fixed** in `public/lists.js`.

**Test-script defects (the product was right, my test was wrong):**
- Run 1 (81/89): A-L-mcbb and A-L-wcbb required `team.name` but poll teams carry `team.short`; B-02, B-04, B-05, E-04 and N-05 matched text case-sensitively although the UI upper-cases headings (CSS `text-transform`); S-02 went offline before the service worker controlled the page (a first visit has nothing cached by design), so it now reloads once online first. 
- Unit-test authoring fixes during development: wrong hand-computed NBA expectation, a path test that looped on `/proc`, a fixture that produced negative wins at 0-0, a stub missing gymnastics, a render-test team object missing `sport`.
- Run 2 (88/89): only B-05 remained, again a case-sensitive text match (the content was correct); fixed.
- Final run: **89/89 pass** on the committed code.

**Not tested / limits:** real iOS Safari (Chromium mobile emulation only); behaviour after Render's free-plan cold start on the new endpoints; the live site (nothing deployed). NCAA.com proxy availability on the day of release is outside our control.
