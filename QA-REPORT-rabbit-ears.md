## What's in this release
**(1) Logo contrast.** Some team logos nearly disappeared on dark surfaces (Justin's example: Broncos › Schedule on the navy team backdrop — the Panthers' black-and-blue panther). Every place the app draws a team logo now measures it in the browser (`public/logotone.js`, canvas, cached per image + surface) and, only when it fails, adds a 1-pixel outline in the opposite tone (`img.lt-lift`, a soft white ring, on dark surfaces; `img.lt-sink`, a soft dark ring, on light). Dark surfaces also prefer ESPN's `500-dark` art where it exists; the team-page header keeps its white tile with the normal art. Logos that pass are untouched, so nothing looks heavier than before.

**The 3:1 rule (WCAG 2.x SC 1.4.11, non-text contrast).** A logo passes on a surface when **both**: (a) at least **50% of its silhouette edge** is at 3:1 or more against the surface — an edge pixel counts if it, or any logo pixel within 2 px of it at 48 px display size, reaches 3:1 (so a thin white keyline around a strong outline isn't "invisible"); and (b) at least **15% of all its pixels** reach 3:1 (so a mark that shows only its keyline — the Panthers on navy: edge 66%, body 9% — still fails). The outline is re-scored after the fix ("after").

**(2) Search.** The favorites/players search now uses a server index of complete lists — every PGA TOUR, LIV, LPGA and DP World Tour player, ESPN's full ATP + WTA athlete list (about 18,000, tagged with live ranks), every roster in NFL, MLB, NBA, WNBA, NHL and the soccer leagues, college teams including all FCS programs, and AUSL — merged with ESPN's athlete search. Matching folds accents and punctuation (Aberg = Åberg, jamarr = Ja'Marr), forgives one or two typos (McElroy → McIlroy, Djokovich → Djokovic, mahomez → Mahomes) with a consonant-skeleton guard against junk matches, and knows team nicknames/abbreviations (Niners, Habs, Bama, Sixers, Talons …). For golf, a well-known player with an ESPN bio outranks a near-namesake (Rory McIlroy above Dermot McElroy).
